FoodRush turns “where should we eat?” into a fun group flow: Room → Decide → Order → Receipt → Split.

CREATE A ROOM
Start a hangout, share one invite link or room code, and friends join in seconds — even as guests, no account needed. Sign up if you want to keep history.

DECIDE TOGETHER
• Everyone suggests restaurants
• Vote as a group
• Pick a mode: race all places, vote with a race on ties, or vote only
• Tied votes? Restaurants race it out — the winner is dinner

ORDER AS A GROUP
Each person adds their own items. The host locks orders and shares one combined list with the restaurant (WhatsApp, call, or delivery app).

SPLIT FAIRLY
Upload the receipt (or skip the photo), add delivery, tax, service, or discount, and everyone sees exactly what they owe. Mark payments until the room is settled.

BUILT FOR REAL LIFE
Office lunches, friend nights, last-minute plans. English & Arabic. Delete your account anytime in Profile or at foodrush.app.

Privacy: we collect account email (if you sign up), display name, room/order data, and receipt photos you upload. We do not sell your data.
