FoodRush is group food ordering made simple.

Create a room, share one invite link, and let friends join in seconds — even as guests, no account required.

Decide what to eat:
• Suggest restaurants
• Vote as a group
• Tied vote? The restaurants race it out

Then order together, upload the receipt, and split the bill so everyone sees exactly what they owe.

Built for hangouts, office lunches, and last-minute “where are we eating?” chaos.

Privacy: we collect account email (if you sign up), display name, room/order data, and receipt photos you upload. We do not sell your data. Delete your account anytime from Profile.
