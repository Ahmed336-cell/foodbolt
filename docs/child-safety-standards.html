FoodRush Child Safety Standards

App: FoodRush (Google Play)

Last updated: 28 August 2026

These are the publicly published child safety standards for FoodRush, the group food-ordering app listed on Google Play. They apply to all users and to all user-generated content in FoodRush rooms (display names, suggestions, orders, notes, and receipt photos).

Zero tolerance: FoodRush strictly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in any form. There are no exceptions.

Prohibition of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

FoodRush explicitly prohibits all of the following:

  • Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) content of any kind
  • Sexualization of minors, grooming, or solicitation of minors
  • Sharing, requesting, or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Using FoodRush to contact, exploit, or endanger children
  • Display names, images, text, or links that sexualize or exploit minors
  • Any attempt to use invite links or rooms to target or harm children

Violations result in immediate account termination, content removal, and reporting to law enforcement and relevant child-protection organizations where required by law.

Protecting children on FoodRush

  • FoodRush is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for use by children
  • Accounts require users to be 13 or older (or the minimum age in their country)
  • Rooms are private and invite-only; there is no public feed or open messaging
  • Hosts can cancel rooms; users can leave rooms at any time
  • We investigate child-safety reports as priority incidents

Reporting CSAE or child safety concerns

If you see content or behavior on FoodRush that involves CSAE, CSAM, grooming, or any risk to a child, report it immediately to our Child Safety Team using the contact below. Include the room code, display names involved, and a description of what you saw.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first, then notify us.
