FoodRush Community Guidelines

App: FoodRush (Google Play)

Last updated: 28 August 2026

These are the externally published standards we use for FoodRush user-generated content (display names, restaurant suggestions, order notes, room names, and receipt photos). For child safety, see our dedicated Child Safety Standards (CSAE prohibited).

What FoodRush is for

FoodRush helps friends decide where to eat, place one group order, and split the bill. Content in rooms should stay related to that purpose.

Allowed content

  • Friendly display names and avatars
  • Restaurant names and food-related suggestions
  • Order items, quantities, prices, and short notes
  • Receipt photos of your group’s bill

Not allowed

  • Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) — zero tolerance (full policy)
  • Hate speech, harassment, threats, or bullying
  • Sexual, pornographic, or exploitative content
  • Illegal activity, scams, fraud, or phishing
  • Violence, terrorism, or dangerous organizations
  • Spam, impersonation, or misleading identities
  • Personal data of others shared without permission
  • Content that violates applicable law

How content is moderated

  • Rooms are invite-based (code or link); they are not a public social feed
  • Hosts can cancel a room; users can leave a room
  • Users may report problems via the support email on the Google Play listing
  • We may remove content, restrict features, or delete accounts that break these standards
  • Serious or illegal abuse may be reported to authorities when required

Reporting

If you see content that breaks these guidelines, contact us through the support email on the FoodRush Google Play page and include the room code and what happened. We aim to review reports promptly.

Child safety / CSAE: report immediately to childsafety@foodrush.app or see Child Safety Standards.

Accounts

You can delete your account anytime from Profile in the app or on our account deletion page.

الغرض

فودراش لمساعدة الأصدقاء على اختيار مكان الأكل وطلب جماعي وتقسيم الفاتورة. المحتوى يجب أن يبقى ضمن هذا الغرض.

غير مسموح

  • خطاب الكراهية أو التحرش أو التهديد
  • محتوى جنسي أو استغلالي
  • نشاط غير قانوني أو احتيال
  • عنف أو إرهاب
  • سبام أو انتحال شخصية
  • مشاركة بيانات الآخرين بدون إذن

الإبلاغ

أبلغ عبر بريد الدعم في صفحة Google Play مع كود الغرفة ووصف المشكلة. قد نزال المحتوى أو نقيّد الحسابات المخالفة.
